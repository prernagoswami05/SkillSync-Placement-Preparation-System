Refund & Cancellation Policy

Platform: SkillSync

Last Updated: 9 November 2026

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how subscription cancellations, refund requests and payment-related issues are handled on SkillSync. Please read this policy before purchasing a Premium subscription.

1. Subscription Plans

SkillSync currently offers the following plans:

  • Free Plan – ₹0
  • Premium Monthly – ₹99 for one month
  • Premium Quarterly – ₹249 for three months

Subscription features and durations are described on our Pricing page.

2. Subscription Cancellation

  • SkillSync Premium subscriptions do not renew automatically.
  • Users are not charged automatically when their subscription expires.
  • Users may choose not to renew their subscription after expiry.
  • Cancellation of future renewal does not automatically mean that an already completed payment will be refunded.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be reviewed under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payment for the same subscription.
  • Payment deducted but Premium access not activated after successful payment verification.
  • Incorrect or unauthorized charges, subject to investigation.
  • Other exceptional circumstances reviewed by SkillSync under applicable law.

Approval depends on verification of the transaction and the circumstances of the request.

4. Situations Where Refunds May Not Be Available

A refund may not be approved merely because:

  • A user changes their mind after purchasing a subscription.
  • A user does not use the Premium features during the subscription period.
  • A user forgets their subscription expiry date.
  • A user has used the purchased service and requests a refund without an eligible reason.

These conditions do not limit any refund or consumer rights available under applicable law.

5. Failed Payments and Amount Deducted

Sometimes a payment may fail even though the amount appears to have been deducted from the user's bank account or payment method.

In such cases, users should first check the payment status and transaction history. If the payment remains unresolved, contact SkillSync support with the transaction reference.

Refunds for failed or reversed transactions are subject to verification and the payment gateway or banking process.

7. Refund Review and Processing

Each refund request will be reviewed after the relevant account and payment details have been verified.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's available process.

The time taken for the refunded amount to appear in the user's account may depend on the bank, payment provider and transaction method. We will communicate relevant updates where available.

8. Payment Disputes

If you notice an incorrect charge or an unfamiliar transaction, contact SkillSync support promptly. We may request relevant transaction information to investigate the issue.
