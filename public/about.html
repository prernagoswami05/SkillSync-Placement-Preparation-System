About SkillSync

Welcome to SkillSync – a placement preparation platform designed to help students prepare for technical assessments, improve their skills and approach placement opportunities with greater confidence.

Who We Are

SkillSync is a student-focused learning and placement preparation platform. It brings assessment practice, performance tracking and structured preparation guidance together in one place.

Our aim is to make preparation more organized by helping students understand their current performance, recognize improvement areas and work towards their learning goals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make placement preparation more accessible, structured and skill-focused. We aim to encourage consistent practice and help students make better use of their preparation time.

Our Vision

We envision a platform where students can understand their strengths, identify skill gaps and follow a more organized approach to technical learning and career preparation.

What SkillSync Offers

1. Technical Assessments

Students can attempt assessments to practice technical concepts and evaluate their preparation.

2. Performance Reports

Assessment reports help students review their scores and understand areas where further improvement may be needed.

3. Preparation Roadmaps

SkillSync provides preparation guidance to help students organize their learning and focus on relevant topics.

4. Topic-wise Practice

Students can strengthen their understanding through focused practice in different technical areas.

5. Company-specific Preparation

Premium users can access additional company-oriented preparation resources and practice features.

Free and Premium Access

SkillSync offers a Free plan with access to selected preparation features. Users can also choose a Premium subscription for additional assessment, practice and performance-analysis features.

For current plans and pricing, visit our Pricing page.

Who Can Use SkillSync?

SkillSync is intended for college students, fresh graduates and learners who want to improve their technical knowledge and prepare for placement assessments and interviews.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide a useful and accessible preparation experience. We continuously seek to improve the platform and its learning resources based on user needs and feedback.

Important Disclaimer

SkillSync is an educational and placement preparation platform. It does not provide a guarantee of employment, internship, interview selection or placement. Individual results depend on a student's skills, preparation and other factors.
