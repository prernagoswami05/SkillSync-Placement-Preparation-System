Contact Us

We're here to help! If you have questions, face technical issues or need assistance with your SkillSync account, you can contact our support team using the details below.

1. General Enquiries

For general questions, platform information, suggestions or feedback about SkillSync, please contact us by email.

2. Account and Login Support

Contact us if you experience difficulties with account registration, login, profile information or account access.

3. Assessment and Technical Support

If assessments are not loading, scores are not displaying correctly or a platform feature is not working as expected, describe the issue and include relevant details so we can investigate.

4. Premium Subscription and Payment Support

For questions about Premium plans, subscription activation, payment status or subscription expiry, contact our support team.

5. Refund and Cancellation Support

For refund requests, duplicate payments or payment-related issues, please review our Refund & Cancellation Policy before contacting us.

6. Privacy-Related Queries

For questions about personal information, data correction, account information or privacy requests, please refer to our Privacy Policy and contact us by email.

7. Email Support

For all support-related queries, write to us at:

prernacodelab@gmail.com

Clicking the email address opens a Gmail compose window with our support email pre-filled as the recipient.

8. Information to Include in Your Email

  • Your registered SkillSync email address.
  • A clear subject describing your issue.
  • A brief explanation of the problem.
  • Relevant transaction reference, if your query concerns payment.
  • A screenshot of the error, if helpful.

For your security, never share your password, OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or complete card details with anyone claiming to provide SkillSync support.

9. Support Response Time

We aim to respond to support enquiries within 2–3 business days. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request.

10. Our Commitment

We aim to review user concerns carefully and provide appropriate assistance regarding platform access, assessments, subscriptions and payment-related issues.
