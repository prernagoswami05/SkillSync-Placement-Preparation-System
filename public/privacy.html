Privacy Policy

Platform: SkillSync

Last Updated: 9 November 2026

SkillSync respects the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you access or use our placement preparation platform. By using SkillSync, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Name provided during account registration.
  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Account identification and profile information.

1.2 Account and Authentication Information

  • Password credentials stored in hashed form.
  • Authentication tokens and account session information.
  • Information required to maintain account security.

1.3 Assessment and Learning Information

  • Assessment answers and submitted responses.
  • Assessment scores and performance reports.
  • Learning progress and topic-wise performance.
  • Activity associated with preparation features.

1.4 Subscription Information

  • Selected subscription plan.
  • Subscription status and expiry information.
  • Payment transaction references and payment status, where available to SkillSync.

Payment card numbers, CVV and banking credentials should be handled by the payment gateway. SkillSync does not intend to store these sensitive payment credentials on its own servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Authenticating users and maintaining account security.
  • Providing assessments and recording submitted responses.
  • Generating performance reports and preparation guidance.
  • Managing subscription plans and Premium access.
  • Verifying payment status and resolving payment-related issues.
  • Improving platform functionality and user experience.
  • Responding to support requests and user queries.
  • Detecting misuse, unauthorized access and security issues.

3. Data Storage and Security

SkillSync uses a database system to store account, assessment and related application information. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Passwords are stored using hashing rather than plain-text storage. However, no online service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate SkillSync, including:

  • Database and hosting service providers.
  • Payment gateway providers for payment processing.
  • Technical service providers supporting platform operations.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Such sharing should be limited to information reasonably required for the relevant service or legal obligation.

5. Cookies and Technical Information

SkillSync may use cookies, authentication tokens or similar technologies where required for login sessions, account security and essential platform functionality.

Browser and technical information may also be processed to maintain and troubleshoot the platform.

6. Data Retention

We retain user information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, manage subscriptions, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymized, subject to applicable retention obligations.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request to:

  • Access personal information associated with their account.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Raise questions about how their information is processed.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests can be submitted through our Contact Us page. We may need to verify the account owner's identity before processing a request.

8. Third-Party Services

SkillSync may depend on third-party services for hosting, database operations and payment processing. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Users should review the relevant third-party policies when using those services.

9. Children's Privacy

SkillSync is intended for students and learners. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information improperly, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our platform, services or legal obligations change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.
