SkillSync Pricing Plans

Choose a plan that supports your learning and placement preparation goals. Start with our Free plan or access additional preparation features with Premium.

1. Free Plan – ₹0

Get started with essential placement preparation features.

  • One complete assessment per week.
  • Basic assessment performance report.
  • Basic preparation roadmap.
  • Limited topic-wise practice.
  • Access to available free learning resources.

Price: Free – no subscription payment required.

2. Premium Monthly – ₹99

Suitable for students who want additional practice and assessment opportunities.

  • Unlimited assessments during the active subscription period.
  • Company-specific practice tests.
  • Advanced performance analytics.
  • Weak-topic identification and focused practice.
  • Additional preparation resources.
  • Access to all included Premium features.

Subscription duration: 1 month.

Price: ₹99 per month.

3. Premium Quarterly – ₹249

A three-month plan for students who prefer a longer preparation period.

  • All included Premium features.
  • Unlimited assessments during the active subscription period.
  • Company-specific preparation tests.
  • Advanced performance analytics.
  • Focused practice and additional resources.

Subscription duration: 3 months.

Price: ₹249 for three months.

Cost comparison: Three monthly payments would cost ₹297. The quarterly plan costs ₹249, saving ₹48.

4. Subscription Activation

Premium access will be activated after successful payment verification by SkillSync. Users should check their account to confirm that their selected subscription plan has been activated.

5. Subscription Expiry and Renewal

  • Monthly Premium access is valid for one month.
  • Quarterly Premium access is valid for three months.
  • Subscriptions do not renew automatically.
  • Users must manually make a new payment to renew after expiry.
  • After expiry, the account returns to Free plan access.

6. Payments

Payments for Premium subscriptions are processed through the payment methods made available on SkillSync. Premium features are subject to successful payment verification.

7. Refund and Cancellation

Please review our Refund and Cancellation Policy before purchasing a subscription. Refund requests are handled according to the conditions described in that policy.

8. Important Information

  • Premium access is linked to the user's SkillSync account.
  • Subscription benefits are available only during the active period.
  • Feature availability may depend on the current platform implementation.
  • SkillSync does not guarantee placement, employment or interview selection.

9. Need Assistance?

For questions about plans, payments or subscriptions, visit our Contact Us page.
